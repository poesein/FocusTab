The lockfile pins installed package versions. `scripts/build.mjs` reads esbuild's actual bundle inputs, copies the corresponding license texts into `dist/licenses/`, and writes `dist/licenses/inventory.json` with exact versions, source metadata and license paths. A missing runtime license causes the build to fail. esbuild's linked legal comments are also preserved next to its generated JavaScript.

Build/test dependencies such as esbuild and jsdom are development tools and are not shipped as extension runtime code. The package lock records their versions and licenses.

## Design research

[Bonjourr](https://github.com/victrme/Bonjourr) informed the evaluation of minimal, customizable new-tab experiences. No Bonjourr source code, artwork, styles, or assets are copied into FocusTab, and Bonjourr is not a dependency. The visual design and implementation in `focus-tab/src` are new work.

No third-party logo, remotely loaded font, image, script or CDN asset is used by the extension pages.
